# Support

For reproducible bugs, [open an issue](https://github.com/masonjames/emdash-paid-access/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
with versions, installation mode, synthetic reproduction steps, expected
behavior, and sanitized evidence. Check [troubleshooting](docs/reference/troubleshooting.md)
first. Community support is best effort; no response-time commitment is implied.

For setup, integration, migration, managed hosting, or ongoing support, contact
Mason James through the [Paid Access page](https://masonjames.com/plugins/paid-access/)
or **hi@masonjames.com**. Scope, price, and any service commitment are agreed separately.

Paid Access is the complete free GPL plugin: no license fee, paid feature tier,
or percentage of creator payments. Stripe memberships and agent reads pay the
creator's own account/wallet; external provider fees may apply. A hosted-service
subscription is not part of this beta.

Do not include credentials, subscriber information, cookies, magic links, or
private keys in public reports. Send suspected access bypasses and other security
issues [privately](SECURITY.md).
